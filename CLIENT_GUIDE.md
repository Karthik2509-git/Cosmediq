# Patient Portal Guide: Managing Your Skincare Journey

Welcome to the **Cosmediq Patient Portal**. This guide is designed to help you navigate your personalized skin and hair treatment records, track your appointments, and communicate with our clinic coordinators.

---

## 1. Portal Overview

When you log in, your personalized dashboard provides a consolidated view of your active skincare journey. The layout is optimized to display the most relevant details clearly:

* **Your Skincare Timeline:** A chronological view of your entire treatment history.
* **Prescriptions & Recommendations:** Your detailed, active medical guidelines.
* **Scanned Reports & Files:** Secure downloads of clinical scans and diagnostic sheets.
* **Payment Ledger:** A clear summary of paid, pending, and partially paid invoices.
* **Quick Callback Actions:** A single-click method to request appointment callback setups.

---

## 2. Navigating Your Medical Timeline

The **Treatment Journey Timeline** provides a detailed record of your progress. It displays:

1. **Date & Speciality:** When the visit occurred and which clinical area was evaluated.
2. **Clinician Name:** The dermatologist or specialist who directed your consultation.
3. **Diagnosis Summary:** Believable medical findings (e.g., *Moderate Inflammatory Acne Vulgaris (Grade II)* or *Epidermal Moisture Impairment*).
4. **Prescription and Directions:** Clear instructions on application frequency and durations (e.g., retinoid creams or barrier recovery moisturizers).
5. **Follow-up Guidelines:** Highly structured recommendation dates for your next checkup.

---

## 3. Scans & Medical Reports

Our doctors upload high-resolution diagnostic files directly to your portal. To access them:

1. Locate the **Medical Files & Scans** card on your portal dashboard.
2. Review the file name and description (e.g., *David_Skin_Moisture_Recovery_Chart.pdf* or *Ananya_Cheek_Pustule_MicroScan.jpg*).
3. Click the secure download link to view or print the file.
4. All downloads are logged on our security trail to protect your private healthcare records.

---

## 4. Invoicing and Payments

To inspect your billing records:

* **Paid Invoices:** View itemized receipts detailing individual treatment components (e.g., *Salicylic Skin Clearing Peel* or *Consultation Fee*), along with the method of payment (CASH, CARD, or UPI) and date of transaction.
* **Pending Invoices:** Displays outstanding balances. Outstanding balances are payable directly to the reception desk during check-in or checkout.

---

## 5. Booking and Callback Requests

To prevent schedule conflicts and maintain medical compliance, **patients cannot directly book confirmed slots**. Instead, all slot allocations are confirmed by our clinic receptionists:

* **Requesting a Callback:** Click the **Request Callback** button on your dashboard.
* **Enter Details:** Select your preferred specialist, approximate date, and a description of your today's complaint.
* **Receptionist Confirmation:** Your request is immediately queued on the receptionist dashboard. Our clinic coordinator will contact you by phone to finalize your booking slot.
